Note: Centroid divergence correction tracks the geometric center of the Gaussian spot at distance z = √(D² + y²), eliminating radial beam-expansion bias.

1. Optical Triangulation Geometry
tan(θ) = y / D  ⇒  θ = tan−1(y / D)

A 650 nm laser emitter mounted on the moving distal flexure arm projects onto a vertical millimeter grid at fixed horizontal distance D. Angular joint flexion θ produces a vertical spot displacement y, enabling non-contact angular measurement without placing mechanical instruments across the joint.

2. Gaussian Spot Divergence
w(z) = w0 √[ 1 + (z / zR)2 ]

As the optical slant path z = √(D² + y²) increases, the laser spot radius w(z) expands relative to the initial beam waist w0 and Rayleigh range zR = πw02/λ. Measuring from the spot’s geometric centroid removes radial expansion error.

3. Biomechanical Vector Dot Product
θinc = cos−1[ (BA · BC) / (|BA| |BC|) ]

Representing proximal landmark A, joint center B, and distal landmark C in calibrated coordinates yields segment vectors BA and BC. Their normalized dot product computes the included anatomical angle and flexion angle (θflex = 180° − θinc).

Physical Therapy & Rehabilitation Monitoring

Enables non-contact tracking of elbow and knee range-of-motion (ROM) during post-injury recovery exercises. Eliminates physical pressure from contact goniometers on sensitive or swollen joints while giving immediate visual feedback on a grid target.

Rural School Ergonomic Posture Screening

Provides an accessible, portable optical setup for screening student seating alignment, forward head tilt, and limb flexion angles in rural schools and community health camps where multi-camera clinical motion labs are unavailable.

System Advantages & Operational Scope

  • Non-contact optical measurement with clear trigonometric geometry.
  • Centroid correction maintains accuracy despite Gaussian beam divergence.
  • Educational prototype intended for supervised biomechanics demonstration.
